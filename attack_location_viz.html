Toy Models of Superposition · attack-location companion

Where you attack matters as much as how hard

The same toy autoencoder — encode n sparse features into m hidden dimensions, then decode back out — can be attacked in two different places for the same L2 budget: at the input, before anything has been compressed, or at the bottleneck, on the already-compressed hidden state, right before decoding. Superposition changes how much damage each of those buys an attacker, and by how much it leaks onto features the attacker never touched.

Features are abstract synthetic directions (Feature 0 … Feature 7) — there's no claim any of them correspond to a human-interpretable concept, in keeping with the toy model's own setup.
